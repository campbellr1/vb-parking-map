    Methods, sources and limitations

    Prepared Nov. 12, 2026. Rates and capacities verified Nov. 5, 2026. Independent compilation from public sources; not an official City of Virginia Beach product.

    Method

    • Parking. 55 locations: city garages and surface lots, Town Center garages, metered and signed street zones, boat ramps, parks, park-and-ride lots and a sample of private lots. Prices are calculated from published rate schedules for the selected date and length of stay.
    • Food and beverage. Virginia Department of Health inspection records, filtered to restaurants, bars, cafes, bakeries and hotel restaurants. Closed sites, schools, care facilities, caterers, commissaries and mobile units are excluded. Outlets are assigned to 18 commercial areas by street address.
    • Retail. Store counts for major shopping centers, assigned to the nearest commercial area.
    • Parking near an area. City garages and public lots within each area's radius, shown in its detail panel. Radii range from 0.6 to 2.8 miles depending on the area's extent, so neighboring areas can share a facility.

    Limitations

    • Base map. The Chart view is simplified from USGS and NOAA reference points, so its shoreline, roads and city limits are approximate and the Town Center street layout is schematic. The Streets view uses OpenStreetMap and the Satellite view uses Esri imagery; both load only where the page is hosted on a standard website. In those views, pin positions are approximate, within about a block at the Oceanfront. Directions links use street addresses and are exact.
    • Rates. Meter rates follow the plan adopted by City Council on Dec. 9, 2025, as the current meter page lists no amounts. Flat event and peak-weekend rates are not modeled. Posted signs govern.
    • Capacity. City figures are used where published. Values marked ~ are estimates or older listings, with the source given in each detail panel. The city lists the 25th Street Garage at 344 spaces, though its stated garage total implies 532.
    • F&B counts. Most records date from 2023 and 2024, so openings and closures since then are not reflected.
    • Retail coverage. Major centers only. Strip centers and street-front shops, including most Oceanfront retail, are excluded, so retail is understated. Red Mill Commons' figure includes restaurants. Town Center is listed by sources at 95 and 146 stores; 95 is used.
    • Not mapped. Hotel, office, school and shopping center customer lots; Resort Area residential permit zones (permit required 6 p.m. to 6 a.m.); Beach Garden Park and Williams Farm Park, whose locations could not be confirmed; and the former 19th Street lots.
    • Resident voucher. Accepted through the HONK app at locations tagged Voucher and at metered spaces around Atlantic Park. The city does not list the 31st Street Garage, Croatan or Sandbridge; Croatan and Sandbridge accept the seasonal permit.
